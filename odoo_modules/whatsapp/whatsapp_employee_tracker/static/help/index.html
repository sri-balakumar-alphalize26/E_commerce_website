1. Overview

This module does two jobs: (a) monitors how employees use the company WhatsApp numbers, and (b) acts as a sales agent — when a customer messages in, the bot suggests products, negotiates price, takes the order, collects the delivery address, takes payment (UPI / Card / Cash on Delivery), and sends the invoice — all on WhatsApp.

Every step is configurable per employee session.

2. First-time Setup

1
Connect WhatsApp: WhatsApp > Sessions → scan QR (status = Connected).
2
Employee Session: WA Tracker > Configuration > Employee Sessions > New → pick Employee + WhatsApp Session + phone.
3
Keep Enable Monitoring ON → Save.
4
Turn on Enable Auto-Reply and set the Checkout options (Section 6).

3. Auto-Reply Bot

If the employee does not reply within the timeout, the bot answers automatically (per-employee, default OFF).

Customer: "i need charger" → product list [Product 1] [Product 2] ... Customer: 1 → product detail + [✅ Order Now] [💬 Negotiate] [🔍 Search Other] Customer: yes → "How many units?" [1] [2] [5] Customer: 2 → Order created → Delivery → Payment
Every step shows buttons + text — tap or type.

4. Price Bargain

After product detail, the customer can tap [💬 Negotiate Price] or type reduce / best price / a number.

RangeDetail
CeilingMRP = "Retail Price (WhatsApp)"
FloorSale Price = "Sales Price" — never below this
Rounds2 offers, then final = sale price

Accept → quantity → the order uses the negotiated price.

5. Customer Types (New vs Saved / B2B)

Make a B2B customer: Contacts > New > Name + Phone (with country code, e.g. +91...) > Save.

⚠ Bot auto-created "ghost" contacts (Internal Notes = "Auto-created from WhatsApp") are still treated as new. Only a manually saved contact gets the B2B price.

7. Payment (UPI / Card / Cash on Delivery)

After the order (and delivery address), the bot shows three options:

📱 UPI → opens a UPI QR page → "I have paid" → done 💳 Card → opens a card form → "Pay" → done 🚲 Cash on Delivery → settled in chat
⚠ TEST / DUMMY mode. The UPI QR and Card pages are simulators (no real money, no Razorpay). The "I have paid / Pay" button completes the test payment. For real UPI/Card, install & configure a real provider (e.g. Razorpay) later.

8. Invoice: Automatic vs Agent-verify

Controlled by the "Automatic Invoice" toggle (Checkout).

⚡ Automatic (toggle ON)

Pay (UPI/Card) → invoice auto create + post + PAID → 📎 paid invoice PDF COD → invoice posted (unpaid) → 📎 invoice PDF

🧑 Agent-verify (toggle OFF)

Pay/COD → Sale Order confirmed → 📎 Order Receipt PDF → "Agent will verify & send your invoice" Agent in 369ai: check payment → Create Invoice → Post → 📎 invoice PDF auto-sent

Agent steps (Agent-verify mode):

1
Sales > Orders → open the WhatsApp order (source "WhatsApp Auto-Reply").
2
Verify the payment is correct.
3
Create Invoice → Post. On posting, the invoice PDF is sent to the customer's WhatsApp automatically.
4
(If paid online) Register Payment as needed.
The invoice is sent only once (no duplicates). COD invoices are posted unpaid — collect payment on delivery.
