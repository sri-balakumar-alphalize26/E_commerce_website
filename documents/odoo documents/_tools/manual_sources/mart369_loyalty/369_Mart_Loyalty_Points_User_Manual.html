369 Mart Loyalty Points

Earn on Every Order, Spend at Checkout
User Manual  |  369ai

What this does

369 Mart Loyalty Points lets customers collect loyalty points on the shop's loyalty card every time they buy, from the shop app, the website or an order your team makes in Sales. Points are added at the moment you choose (by default when the order is Delivered), and the customer can take them off a later order at checkout, as a discount on the bill.

It is one card per customer, found by their mobile number, so points from the store counter and from online orders sit together. The card itself, the points rule and the card screens come from the Loyalty Card module. This module does the earning, the spending and the giving back when an order is cancelled or returned, and it sends no messages of its own.

Part 1 — Setting Up

Done once by a Manager or Owner. If loyalty is already on and the points rule suits you, skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Open 369 Mart → Loyalty from the main menu. These screens come from the Loyalty Card module; this module moves them here, so the card no longer has a separate app of its own in the main menu:

  • Loyalty Cards — every customer's card, with its number, status and points.
  • Customer Details — customers with a mobile number, and their card details.
  • Points History — every points movement. This module adds an Order column, so you can see which order each line belongs to.
  • Points Rules — the points rule (Managers only).
  • Loyalty Settings — the shop-wide loyalty switch, mobile number format and card design (Managers only).
  • Email Settings — the account card emails are sent from (Managers only).
  • Card Images and Download Card Images (ZIP) — the printed card pictures.

The online switches and the Points are earned when choice are not in the backend. There is no backend screen for them: they are set in the staff console, under Settings → Loyalty. (The backend's Store → Settings screen has no Loyalty tab.)

Good to know: This manual covers only what happens with orders. For the card screens themselves (card statuses, the card design, emails, deleting and restoring cards, card images) see the Loyalty Card user manual.

2Switch loyalty on, in both places

Two switches must be on before anything is earned or spent.

  • Enable Loyalty (master) — in the backend, 369 Mart → Loyalty → Loyalty Settings. The shop-wide switch for the whole loyalty card. On by default.
  • Points on orders — in the staff console, Settings → Loyalty. App orders and orders made in Sales earn points on the customer's card. On by default.
  • Spend points at checkout — in the same console tab. Customers can take points off an app order, within the points rule, once a day. On by default. Off: customers keep earning but cannot spend.

Press Save changes at the top of the console screen after changing the Loyalty tab.

Important: If Enable Loyalty (master) is off, nothing is earned or spent online either, whatever the console says. The console's Loyalty tab then shows a note: Loyalty is switched off for the whole shop…. Customers see Loyalty points are resting; their points are kept.

3Choose when points are earned

In the staff console, Settings → Loyalty, pick one option under Points are earned when. Whichever you choose, a cancelled order keeps no points and a refund takes back its share.

OptionWhen an app order earns
Order placedAs soon as it is paid for, or cash on delivery is accepted. A cancellation takes the points back.
Packed or shippedOnce the store has packed it (a Quick order) or shipped it (an Express order).
Out for deliveryWhen it leaves with the rider.
Delivered (default)When the customer has it. Cancelled orders never earn.
Return window closedA set number of days after delivery, once a return is no longer likely. Returns before then simply earn less.
  • Days after delivery — shown only with Return window closed. A whole number from 1 to 90; default 7. Quick picks: 3, 7, 10, 14 and 30.
Good to know: Orders made in Sales have no packing or rider steps. For them, Order placed means when the order is confirmed; every other option means when its delivery is done (all its outgoing deliveries validated).
Tip: The later the step, the fewer points you take back after cancellations and returns. Delivered is a safe middle choice.

4Check the points rule

The rule is changed in one place only: 369 Mart → Loyalty → Points Rules. Only one rule is active at a time. The console's Loyalty tab shows it for reading, under The points rule. This module uses its fields like this:

  • Spend Amount and Points Earned — spend this much, earn that many points. Default ₹100 → 10 points.
  • Min Points to Redeem — the customer needs at least this many points to use any. Default 100.
  • Points per ₹1 — how many points take ₹1 off. Default 10.
  • Max Redeem % — the largest share of an order points may pay. Default 100. Left at 0, it counts as 100%.
Watch out: With no points rule at all, no order earns and checkout offers no points. The console shows There is no points rule yet.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

Once Part 1 is done, points move on their own as orders move. These steps explain what happens, so you can answer customers' questions.

1How a customer gets a card

There is nothing to create by hand. The first time one of a customer's orders earns points, the card is found or made:

  • Already linked — a card already linked to the customer is used.
  • Same mobile number — otherwise a card with the customer's mobile number is used, for example one given out at the store counter, and it is linked to them.
  • No card yet — a new Active card is made with their name, mobile number and email. It is made quietly: no welcome message is sent.

Opening a customer's profile or the points page never makes a card. Until their first order earns, the customer's points page says their card starts with their first order, on the mobile number in their profile.

Watch out: A customer with no mobile number gets no card and earns no points. Add the number to their profile; later orders will then earn.

2How points are earned

When an order reaches the step chosen in Part 1, the points are added to the card once. They are worked out on what the items cost after any coupon and points discount, without the delivery charge, less anything already refunded.

Example, with the default rule (₹100 → 10 points): items of ₹500, a ₹20 coupon and ₹30 delivery earn on ₹480, which is 48 points.

An order earns only once, even if it later passes the same step again. An order that is cancelled, or never paid for, earns nothing. A card that is not Active earns nothing.

3How points are spent at checkout

A signed-in customer with points sees a Loyalty points row at checkout, with their balance and how much they can save. When they tick it, the bill shows a Loyalty points line with a minus amount, just like a coupon. The points rule decides how many can be used:

  • Minimum — the card must hold at least Min Points to Redeem.
  • Value — every Points per ₹1 points take ₹1 off.
  • Maximum share — no more than Max Redeem % of the order total, and never below zero.
  • Once a day — one spend per card per calendar day, counting the store counter too.

The customer does not type a number: the row offers the largest amount allowed, and they use it or not. If points cannot be used, the row says why (see the reference table). The order then shows a line named Loyalty points (N), where N is the points spent.

Good to know: Going back to change the delivery slot is not a second spend. Only a paid order counts toward the once-a-day limit.

4Points held on unpaid orders

Points used at checkout leave the card as soon as the order is placed, even before payment, so the same points cannot go on a second basket. While the order is unpaid the hold is soft:

  • Placing again — if the customer goes back and places the order again, the held points return first and are taken again.
  • Never paid — an hourly automatic job gives back points held by an unpaid basket left untouched for more than 24 hours. The line is removed, not shown as a refund.
  • Paid later after all — the points are taken again when the order is placed; if fewer are left on the card, only what is left is taken.

5Cancellations, returns and removed items

Points follow the money:

  • Earned points — any refund (a cancellation refund, a return, an item taken out of the order) takes back a share of the points the order earned, in proportion to the amount refunded. Taking out an item on a cash-on-delivery order refunds nothing but still lowers the points.
  • Spent points — come back to the card only when the order is cancelled or refunded in full. A partial return already refunds the item at its full price, so no share of the spent points comes back.
  • Cancelled order — keeps nothing it earned and loses nothing it spent.
Watch out: The card never goes below zero. If the customer already spent the points an order earned, only what is left on the card is taken back.

6Orders made in Sales

Orders your team creates in Sales earn on the same card, by the same rule. They earn when confirmed (with Order placed) or when their delivery is done (any other option, or after the return window). Cancelling the order takes its points back. Posting a credit note takes back a share in proportion to the amount credited. Customers spend points only at the app or website checkout, not on Sales orders.

  • Checkout — the Loyalty points row, the minus line on the bill, and You'll earn N points… with the step you chose.
  • Order page — points used on the bill, You'll earn… or You earned N loyalty points on this order, and after a cancellation, how many points are back on the card.
Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1The two automatic jobs

JobWhat it does
Daily: return windowOnly when Return window closed is chosen. Adds points to orders delivered at least Days after delivery ago; an order with an open return waits. It looks back only 30 days, so old orders are not paid out.
Hourly: unpaid basketsGives back points held by baskets left unpaid for more than 24 hours.

Both run on their own. A problem with points never stops an order from moving on.

2One card per person

A card is matched to a customer first, and by mobile number only if they have no card. A card that already belongs to someone else is never used, even when the number matches: the number may have changed hands, but the points did not. In that case the customer gets no card and earns nothing until the numbers are sorted out in Loyalty Cards.

Important: The same points can never be spent twice at the same moment. If the balance changed in between, the customer sees Your points balance has just changed. Please check the bill again.

4Messages and privacy

This module sends no SMS, email or WhatsApp. A card made by an order is made silently, even when card emails are switched on in Loyalty Settings. Customers see only their own card and history.

Quick tips

  • Loyalty works only with Enable Loyalty (master) and the console's Points on orders both on.
  • Points are earned when is set only in the staff console, under Settings → Loyalty.
  • Change the rule in one place: Loyalty → Points Rules.
  • No mobile number in the profile means no card and no points.
  • Earned points are taken back in proportion to refunds; spent points come back only on a full cancel or refund.
  • Points are earned on items after discounts, never on the delivery charge.
  • Use the Order column in Points History to trace any movement.
  • Points on an unpaid basket return to the card within about a day.
