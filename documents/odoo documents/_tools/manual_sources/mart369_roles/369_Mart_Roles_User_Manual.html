369 Mart Roles

Who Does What — Owner, Manager, Packer, Accountant & Rider
User Manual  |  369ai

What this does

369 Mart Roles gives every staff member one simple role that says what job they do: User, Packer, Manager or Owner. Two extra ticks, Accountant and Rider, can be added on top, because one person can pack orders and also do the books. The role replaces the backend's own “User / Administrator” line at the top of each person's user form.

The role says what someone may do; the backend's Allowed Companies (the shops they work in) says where. The Owner runs the whole team from one screen, Staff & roles, where they can also invite new staff by email. Only the Owner can hand out roles, and the shop can never be left without an Owner.

How the manual is arranged

Part 1 – Setting Up shows where roles live, what happens on install, and how to set a role on the user form. Part 2 – Everyday Use walks the Owner through the Staff & roles screen: finding someone, changing their role and inviting new staff. Part 3 – Keeping It Secure & In Order covers who may change roles, the “always one Owner” rule and what each role is for.

Part 1 — Setting Up

Done once by the Owner. If your team already has the right roles, skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Roles can be set in two places, and both always agree, because they change exactly the same rights:

  • Settings → Users & Companies → Users — each person's user form, with the Role line and the Accountant and Rider ticks at the top. The users list also shows a Role column.
  • 369 Mart → Store → Staff & roles — one screen for the whole team: roles, ticks, shops and invitations.

The Staff & roles menu is shown to the Owner only. The staff console has the same Staff & roles page under Store, also for the Owner only; anyone else who opens it sees “Only the Owner can manage staff”.

2What happens when you install

So that nobody loses access on the day the module goes in, roles are given out automatically:

  • Administrators — everyone with Administrator (Settings) rights becomes Owner.
  • Website designers — any other staff member with Website designer rights becomes Manager.
  • Everyone else — stays User until you give them a role.
Tip: After installing, open Staff & roles and look at the counts on the role cards. They show at a glance who was given which role.

3Set a role on the user form

Open Settings → Users & Companies → Users and open a staff member. At the top of the form:

  • Role — pick one: User, Packer, Manager or Owner. User is a plain backend login with no 369 Mart job.
  • Accountant — tick if they also handle payments, wallets, invoices, refunds and reports.
  • Rider — tick if they also deliver orders.
  • Allowed Companies — the backend's own field on the same form: which shops they work in.

Save the form as usual. The role, the ticks and the shops are independent: changing one never changes the others.

Watch out: Owner also carries the full Administrator (Settings) right. Moving someone from Owner to any other role takes their administrator rights away, just as the old “User” choice did.
Good to know: The Role line and the ticks are hidden on customer (portal) accounts, because shoppers are not staff. Lower on the same form there is also a 369 Mart Seller line for outside sellers; it does not open any screens yet.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

For the Owner. Everything here is also on the staff console's Staff & roles page, with the same data.

1The Staff & roles screen

Open 369 Mart → Store → Staff & roles. The heading reads “Who works at 369 Mart and what each person may do.” Every staff member is listed here; customers are not.

2Change someone's role

Click a person's row. A panel opens on the right with their name and email. Fill in:

  • Role - pick one — User, Packer, Manager or Owner, each with its short description.
  • Also does - tick any — Accountant (“Payments, wallets, invoices, refunds and reports.”) and Rider (“Delivers orders and closes them with the customer's code.”).
  • Works in — a tick per shop. This only appears when you have more than one shop (company). Keep at least one ticked.

Press Save. A message confirms it, for example “Saved: Asha is now Manager.”, and the list refreshes. Press Cancel, or click outside the panel, to close it without changes. If something is refused, the reason shows in red inside the panel and nothing is saved.

Tip: To promote a new Owner and step down yourself, first make the other person Owner and save, then change your own row.

3Invite a new staff member

Press Invite staff. The panel asks for:

  • Name — their full name (at least two letters).
  • Email — “They sign in with this”. It must not already belong to anyone.
  • Role - pick one — starts on Packer; change it if needed.
  • Also does - tick any — Accountant and/or Rider.

Press Send invite. The person is added straight away and the backend sends its own “set your password” email. The message reads “Asha added. An email to set a password is on its way.” Until they sign in, their Last sign-in says Not signed in yet.

Watch out: If the backend has no outgoing mail server, the person is still added but no email reaches them, even though the message says one is on its way. Ask whoever looks after the backend's email to check it.
Good to know: The invite panel has no Works in list. The new person starts in your current shop; open their row afterwards to change their shops.

4Riders

Only staff with the Rider tick appear in an order's Rider list, so give the tick before handing orders to someone. When 369 Mart WhatsApp Bridge is installed, ticking Rider also links the person to a rider record used by the rider app. That record needs the person's mobile number; without one, no rider record is made yet, though orders can still be handed to them. A new rider starts off duty. Taking the tick away switches their rider record off; giving it back later brings back the same record with its history.

Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1Only the Owner hands out roles

Roles decide who may touch money and settings, so nobody below Owner may change them. The Staff & roles menu is hidden from everyone else, and even if someone reaches the screen another way, the backend refuses with “Only the Owner can change staff and roles.” A non-Owner also cannot see the staff list itself.

When 369 Mart WhatsApp Bridge is installed, every Owner with a mobile number also gets a WhatsApp message each time a staff member signs in to the staff console.

2There is always an Owner

The last remaining Owner cannot be moved down. Staff & roles refuses with: “name is the only Owner. Make someone else Owner first, otherwise nobody could change roles any more.” Make a second Owner first, then change the first one.

Important: This guard is on the Staff & roles screen. Change Owners there rather than on the user form, so the shop is never left without someone who can manage staff.

3What each role is for

These are the jobs each role and tick is meant for, in the words the module uses:

RoleWhat it is for
UserA backend login with no 369 Mart job.
PackerSees the orders waiting to be packed, packs them and marks them packed. No prices to change, no customer lists, no settings, no money.
ManagerRuns the shop day to day: orders, returns, products, stock, customers, offers, delivery and support; approves sellers' products. Can look at money screens but not change payment settings or staff.
OwnerEverything, in 369 Mart and every seller: payment methods, the cash-on-delivery limit, reward amounts, store details, and who has which role.
Accountant (tick)Payments, wallets, invoices, refunds and reports. Can look at orders but not change orders, products or settings.
Rider (tick)Only their own deliveries: close each with the customer's code and record the cash collected.

Quick tips

  • Pick one role; add Accountant or Rider as ticks on top.
  • Role says what someone does; Works in (Allowed Companies) says where.
  • Each role includes the one before: Packer, then Manager, then Owner.
  • Owner is also Administrator (Settings); moving someone off Owner removes it.
  • Always make a second Owner before stepping down.
  • Give the Rider tick before trying to hand orders to someone.
  • An invited person is added even if the email does not arrive.
  • The user form and Staff & roles always agree; use whichever is handier.
