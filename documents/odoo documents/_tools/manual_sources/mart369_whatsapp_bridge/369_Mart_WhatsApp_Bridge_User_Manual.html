369 Mart WhatsApp Bridge

One Shop for the Website and WhatsApp Selling
User Manual  |  369ai

What this does

369 Mart sells through two doors: the website (and shop app), and the WhatsApp selling flow, where customers order in a WhatsApp chat or group. Each door could run a whole shop on its own. 369 Mart: WhatsApp Bridge joins them into one shop: one list of orders, one customer per phone number, one catalogue and one price.

It also lets the shop speak on WhatsApp: order updates in your own words, the invoice, the door code for the rider, sign-in codes, and an alert to the Owner every time a staff member signs in to the console.

How the manual is arranged

Part 1 – Setting Up covers the WhatsApp tab in Settings and the WhatsApp parts of the Delivery settings page. Part 1 — Setting Up

Done once by a Manager or Owner. Everything is switched on by default with the shop's own wording, so you only need this part to change it.

1Where everything lives

The module adds no new menu. It adds to screens you already have, under 369 Mart in the main menu:

  • Store → Settings → WhatsApp tab — which number speaks, the six update messages and the invoice switch.
  • Sales → Orders — a Channel filter and a WhatsApp line on each WhatsApp order.
  • Store → Bot answers — one extra answer, Who is delivering their order.
  • Store → Delivery settings — the WhatsApp parts of the one-page delivery settings (step 5).

The Settings screen needs Website designer or Administrator (Settings) rights, which Managers and Owners have.

2Choose the number that sends updates

Open Store → Settings and click the WhatsApp tab. At the top:

  • Send order updates from — pick a WhatsApp session, or leave Whichever is connected (the default). A coloured label shows its state: green when connected, orange otherwise.
  • Check now — asks the WhatsApp panel for the session's state right now, instead of showing the last known one.
  • Authorized number — the number this shop may pair, and the number actually paired if it differs.
Good to know: The number itself is set on the WhatsApp panel, not here. Switching this shop off on the panel stops these updates too, and the panel's no-reply footer, when ticked, is added to every message.
Tip: If the session is offline, messages are not lost. They wait in the queue and go out when the number is back.

3The six update messages

Below the session there is one switch per order step: Order placed, Packed, Shipped, Out for delivery, Delivered and Cancelled. All are on by default. When a switch is on, a box shows the words sent. Change them as you like; empty the box to go back to the shop's own wording.

Three placeholders fill in details for you. Type them exactly as shown:

PlaceholderReplaced by
%(ref)sThe order number, e.g. 369M-9001.
%(eta)sThe delivery time promised for the order.
%(link)sThe link to the order's tracking page.

Press Save changes. If a placeholder is mistyped, saving stops and names the step to fix, so a broken message is never sent.

Watch out: For an order with a rider job, the Shipped, Out for delivery and Delivered messages come from the delivery job in its own words (the same journey a WhatsApp customer hears). Your switches still apply, but your wording for those three steps is not used.

4The invoice on delivery

Send the invoice PDF when an order is delivered is on by default. When an order is delivered, the customer also gets the invoice as a PDF, captioned Your 369 Mart invoice. It is sent only if the Delivered switch is on, the customer asked for WhatsApp updates, and the invoice has been confirmed.

5Delivery settings: the WhatsApp parts

Open Store → Delivery settings (in the staff console: Delivery → Settings). The page itself is explained in the Delivery & Pricing manual. This module adds the WhatsApp side, so one Save all sets the website and WhatsApp delivery together:

  • Branches — two more columns. WhatsApp shop: made automatically, yes (one a person made), archived, or — (none yet). Riders: how many riders serve the branch, or none in amber. When you save a branch's map pin or Quick, they are written to its WhatsApp delivery shop too, so the branch and its shop never point at two places.
  • Fees and Delivery slots — with the WhatsApp Quick or Express module installed, the page shows and saves that module's fee rules, and its slot list is that module's slots, because those are the ones the website checkout and the WhatsApp order page then offer. Those slots have one name each, so the list has no Detail column. Add a slot adds one to that module, and Save all saves it with the rest of the page. The website's copy of the fees is kept the same.
  • WhatsApp delivery — Promise for WhatsApp orders, what the customer is told when the order is confirmed: Quick within (hours) (1 to 72), If ordered before (hour, 0-23) (after that hour, the next day) and Express in (days) (1 to 60).
  • Call a rider automatically when packed — off: the counter presses "Call a rider". On: the nearest free rider is asked the moment the order is packed.
  • Ask for a review after delivery — adds one line to the customer's private Delivered message, How was it? Rate your products: with a link, once per order and never in a group. On by default.
  • Riders — how many riders there are and how many are on duty, with a Riders & Couriers button. A rider with no branch serves every branch.
Good to know: When a branch sets itself up, every branch on the map also gets its own WhatsApp delivery shop, made once with the branch's pin. A shop someone made or archived is never changed, except that an empty pin is filled from its branch. A delivery job then leaves from the automatically made shop of the branch its stock leaves from. The branch status adds WhatsApp delivery shop and the riders, and says Needs you with Add a rider for this branch when there is none: riders are the one thing that cannot be made automatically.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

What staff see on the Orders screens, and what customers receive on WhatsApp.

1Orders from both channels

Every order now carries a Channel: WhatsApp when it grew out of a WhatsApp chat or group, otherwise Website. Both kinds show on the same Orders screens.

  • Orders desk — a Channel picker (Both channels, Website, WhatsApp). A WhatsApp order's row says WhatsApp and its job number. Opening it shows WhatsApp delivery with the job, its stage, the shop, the rider and the group.
  • Orders list (all views) — Website and WhatsApp filters, and Channel under Group By. The order form shows the Channel and the Delivery job.
Good to know: A WhatsApp order has no 369M- number. It shows under its own order name, such as S00042. Only confirmed WhatsApp orders appear; enquiries that never became orders stay off the board, just like unpaid website baskets.

Moving an order on the Orders desk moves its delivery job, and when a rider or shop moves the job, the order follows. Website orders become rider-app jobs with the right delivery kind and the right cash to collect.

2Who gets the update messages

The six messages from Part 1 go to website customers who ticked WhatsApp at checkout and left WhatsApp on in their account. They go to the customer's own phone (the account's number), not the delivery address's, which may belong to a neighbour.

Customers who ordered on WhatsApp are talked to by the selling flow itself in their own chat, step by step, so they are never messaged twice.

3The door code on WhatsApp

A website order already has a door code, shown in the customer's app since payment. When the rider sets off, the same code is sent on WhatsApp: Your order is arriving. Give this code to the rider after you have the parcel… If someone else is receiving the parcel, the phone on the delivery address gets it too. Whatever the rider types is checked against that one code.

Important: The door code is sent even to customers who switched WhatsApp updates off, because without it there is no delivery. If WhatsApp fails, the customer can still read the code in their app.

4Signing in with a WhatsApp code

When a customer signs in with their mobile number, the sign-in code arrives on WhatsApp from the shop's number: Your 369 Mart code is …. It expires in 10 minutes and must not be shared. The sign-in screen tells the customer honestly whether the code was sent, is waiting to send, or could not be sent.

  • WhatsApp-only customers — someone who has only ever ordered on WhatsApp can sign in with no sign-up. Their account is made on the record that already holds their orders.
  • One number, one customer — once a number is proven, other WhatsApp contacts with the same number are joined into the account: their orders, address, points, wallet and chats.
  • Never joined — staff, companies, riders, shops, supplier-only contacts, and anyone who already has a login.

5Refunds on WhatsApp orders

WhatsApp orders now get money back the same way website orders do:

  • Cancelled before payment — its unpaid bill is cancelled.
  • Cancelled, returned, or refunded after payment — what was paid goes into the customer's 369 Wallet, with a credit note, exactly once.

The customer is told in their own WhatsApp chat: … has been refunded to your 369 Wallet for order … Sign in to 369 Mart with this number to use it. A WhatsApp-only customer's wallet waits for the day they sign in with that number.

6The WhatsApp order menu

The WhatsApp NEW ORDER menu lists the same categories as the website, in the same order, and shows only published products at the website price. Bargaining still works; it just starts from the price the website shows.

When a vendor quotes for something out of stock, the product is still created so it can be offered, but it stays unpublished on the website until somebody prices and publishes it properly.

Tip: To sell something on WhatsApp, publish it on the website first. An unpublished product does not appear in the WhatsApp menu.

7"Who is delivering my order?"

The support bot gets one new answer, Who is delivering their order, under Store → Bot answers. When a customer asks about the rider, driver or delivery person, the bot replies for the order a rider is on. With no order on the way it says You have no orders on the way right now.

8Delivery slots on the WhatsApp order page

The page a WhatsApp customer opens from the link to sign and pay for an order shows a Delivery slot card, marked optional. It offers No preference (As soon as we can, the default) and the same slots the website checkout offers for that order's Quick or Express delivery.

  • No slot fee — picking a slot never changes the order's total, even for a slot that has an extra fee on the website.
  • Only before it is settled — the card shows while the order is still a quotation and not paid. After that the slot cannot be changed.
  • Slots switched off — when the company does not offer delivery slots, there is no card at all.
  • A slot that filled up or closed — the customer is told "That delivery slot is not available any more. Please pick another."
  • A saved slot no longer offered — it is cleared from the order, No preference is chosen again, and the card says Your earlier slot "…" is no longer available. Pick another, or leave it as no preference.

9The tracking map

The customer's tracking page and the office's Delivery → Fleet → Live Map now always show a map. When the delivery job has no pin for the shop, the map uses the pin of the branch the order leaves from (set on Delivery settings). For the customer it uses the pin on the order's delivery address. With neither, the map shows an approximate place from the address, or centres on the customer's town. The Live Map also shows branches that have a pin but no WhatsApp delivery shop.

Good to know: These pins are only for the picture. The route, the arrival time, the rider's pay and which rider is called still use the job's real positions.
Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1The Owner's sign-in alert

Every time a staff member signs in to the staff console, each active Owner with a mobile number gets a WhatsApp message: 369 Mart console sign-in, with who signed in, how, when, from which internet address and on which device. It ends: Not expected? Change their password and the console address.

Watch out: The alert never blocks a sign-in. If WhatsApp is down, the staff member is still let in and no alert arrives. Make sure every Owner has a mobile number on their contact.

2Who can do what

RoleWhat they can do
SalespersonSees and works orders from both channels on the Orders screens.
Website designer (Manager)The Store → Settings → WhatsApp tab: session, messages and invoice switch. The Store → Delivery settings page, WhatsApp parts included.
Administrator (Settings) (Owner)Everything above. Owners also receive the sign-in alert.

3Privacy

Updates go to the customer's own number, not to whoever is at the delivery address; that phone only receives the door code. Accounts are joined only after the customer has proven the number with a code, and staff, riders, shops and existing logins are never joined.

4Removing the module

Uninstalling separates the two shops again. The Orders board goes back to website orders only, and nothing in either the website or the WhatsApp selling flow was changed, so both keep working on their own. Customers already joined, and refunds already paid into the 369 Wallet, stay as they are.

Quick tips

  • Press Check now on the WhatsApp tab whenever customers say updates stopped.
  • Leave a message box empty to keep the shop's own wording.
  • Type placeholders exactly: %(ref)s, %(eta)s, %(link)s.
  • Use the Channel picker on the Orders desk to see only WhatsApp orders.
  • Publish a product on the website before expecting it in the WhatsApp menu.
  • Give every Owner a mobile number so the sign-in alert reaches them.
  • Set a branch's map pin on Delivery settings: its WhatsApp delivery shop follows.
