Signage Scan

Banners + In-Store Price Checker
User Manual  |  369ai

What this does

Signage Scan turns any spare screen — a wall-mounted TV, a tablet, a phone at the counter — into a full-screen display that scrolls through your banners (images or videos) and, the moment a shopper scans a product barcode, shows that product's details before returning to the banners. One screen does two jobs: advertising while idle, a price checker on demand.

An administrator decides which product fields a scan shows — cost price is off by default — and the detail is filtered inside Odoo, so hidden fields never leave the server.

How the manual is arranged

Signage Scan has two sides, and this manual follows them. Part 1 – Setting Up is done once in Odoo, from the Signage Scan menu. Part 2 – Everyday Use is the screen itself and the Manage screens on the terminal. Part 3 – Keeping It Secure & In Order locks the counter down and keeps the catalogue tidy.

Part 1 — Setting Up

Done in Odoo by an administrator. If your products and banners are already in place, skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Open Signage Scan from the Odoo main menu. Everything for setup sits under it:

  • Products — the catalogue the price checker reads (name, barcode, price).
  • Banners — the images and videos that loop on screen.
  • Scan Fields → Per Product / Same Fields for All — which product details a scan shows.
  • Scanner & Display — how long a scanned detail stays on screen.
  • Scan History — every barcode scanned, including ones that matched nothing.
  • Live Preview — a phone-frame preview of the display, inside Odoo.

The whole menu is hidden from ordinary users — only someone with Settings / Administrator rights sees it. (An App Lock entry also exists, but only in developer mode — see Part 3.)

2Add your products

Open Products and add one row per item. The price checker needs only three things:

  • Name — what the product is called.
  • Barcode — exactly what the scanner reads. This is the key the scan looks up.
  • Sales Price — the price shown on scan by default.

A category, internal reference, unit of measure, cost and image are all optional. This is the same catalogue as the app's Manage → Products screen and the full Odoo product form, so a product edited in any one of them shows the change everywhere.

Tip: The barcode must match exactly what the scanner sends. If a scan shows nothing, the barcode is the first thing to check — Scan History will list the code as Not found.

3Create banners

Open Banners and press New. Each banner is one image or one video shown full-screen. Fill in:

  • Name — an internal label for the banner.
  • Media Type — Image or Video.
  • Image / Video — the artwork. An image must be an image; a video must be a real video file (MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V…). An audio file — such as a WhatsApp .ogg voice note — is refused.
  • Time on Screen — Auto or Custom. Auto: images use the app default and a video plays its full length. Custom: stays exactly the seconds you set.
  • Sequence — the order banners play in (drag to reorder).
  • Show on display — the simple on/off. Off hides it from the live screen but keeps it in your list.
  • Start / End Date — an optional schedule; the banner is Live only inside the window.
Watch out: For a video, keep Time on Screen on Auto. With Custom, a video longer than the seconds set is cut off at that time — it does not play faster, it just stops early.
Good to know: Show on display is not archiving. Turning it off hides the banner from the screen but keeps it in your list, ready to switch back on. Archive (the Active flag) is what removes a banner from the list entirely.

4Choose what a scan shows — per product

Open Scan Fields → Per Product. Each product carries a master Show on scan toggle right in the list. Open one to choose its detail:

  • Show on scan — off means scanning this barcode shows nothing at all.
  • Scan: show image — show the product photo on the detail.
  • Fields to show on scan — every product field with its own on/off toggle. The name always shows; Sales Price and the sale description are on by default; cost price is off by default. Drag to reorder.

Custom fields you add to products in Odoo appear in this list automatically, switched off — so a new field is never exposed on the screen by surprise.

Important: A field that is off is filtered out on the server — its value is never sent to the screen. So cost price (off by default) can't be read even by inspecting the app.

5Same fields for all products

If every product should show the same details, open Scan Fields → Same Fields for All and turn on “Same fields for all products”. Choose the shared field list once; it then applies to every scanned product and overrides the per-product lists.

ModeWhat a scan uses
Off (default)Each product shows its own Per Product field list.
OnEvery product shows the one shared list you pick here, whatever its own list says.
Important: While Same fields for all products is on, changes to a single product's own fields won't appear on scan until you turn it off. Odoo warns you if you edit a product's fields while Global mode is on. The master Show on scan per product still applies in both modes.

6Scanner & Display timing

Open Scanner & Display and set Detail display time — how long a scanned product's details stay up before the banners come back — in seconds or minutes. It applies to every screen.

Tip: The scan method — USB scanner or phone camera — is not set here. It's a per-device choice made on each terminal under Manage → Scanner & Display, because it depends on that screen's hardware.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

On the screen and the terminal at the counter. Once Part 1 is done, this is all staff touch.

1The display

Signed in, the app shows your banners full-screen, in sequence, looping. Only Live banners appear — those with Show on display on and within any schedule set. There is nothing to do; it runs on its own.

2Scanning a product

Point the phone camera at a barcode, or fire a USB scanner. The matching product's details slide in — name, image (if enabled) and the fields you chose — and stay up for the Detail display time, then the banners resume.

If a barcode matches no product, or the product has Show on scan off, the screen simply returns to the banners. Either way the scan is recorded (see Scan History) so you can spot gaps.

Good to know: Every scan is logged, and repeats are de-duplicated — a barcode held in front of the camera for a few seconds doesn't fill the history with copies.

3Manage on the terminal

A screen signed in as a manager has a Manage section — the same setup, on the device itself:

Manage screenWhat it does
OverviewCounts of banners (total, live, video) and products (total, on scan).
BannersAdd, edit, reorder, turn on/off and delete banners as cards.
ProductsCreate and edit products — name, barcode, price, category, image and custom fields.
Scan FieldsThe per-product and “same fields for all” field choices.
Scanner & DisplayThe scan method for this device, plus the detail display time.
Scan HistoryRecent scans, with a Clear button for managers.

Everything here mirrors the Odoo side: a change made on the terminal shows in Odoo, and the other way round.

4Scan modes — set per device

Under Manage → Scanner & Display, pick how this screen reads barcodes:

  • USB scanner — a hardware scanner plugged into a TV or box; it types the code like a keyboard.
  • Mobile camera — the phone or tablet camera reads the barcode.

The choice is stored on the device, so a counter phone and a wall-mounted TV can each use whatever suits them.

5Preview before you go live

In Odoo, Signage Scan → Live Preview opens a phone-frame preview of exactly what the screen will show — videos and all — without needing a second device. Use it to check a new banner or the running order before it reaches the shop floor.

Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1Lock the Manage screens (App Lock)

The screens at the counter run signed in as an administrator, so anyone holding the device could open Manage. A PIN sits in front of Manage — administrators included.

Turn it on in Odoo under Signage Scan → App Lock (this menu shows only in developer mode). Set a 4-digit PIN — numbers only, because the terminal enters it on a numeric keypad — and choose what it guards:

Ask for a PINWhat it guards
No PINManage opens normally.
Whole Manage sectionOne PIN in front of everything.
Selected options onlyPick which of Products, Banners, Scan Fields, Scanner & Display and Scan History need it.

Auto-lock after a set number of seconds of no tapping (minimum 5) re-locks Manage on its own; every tap restarts the countdown, so it never interrupts someone mid-edit.

Watch out: Locking Scanner & Display also locks the Scan mode shortcut on the app's Profile screen — otherwise the lock is walked around that way. Shop staff would then need the PIN to switch between the USB scanner and the camera.
Important: The PIN guards the counter; it does not change Odoo rights. A screen signed in as an administrator still has administrator rights on the server — the PIN just stops a bystander opening Manage. The PIN itself never leaves the server (only a hash is stored), and five wrong tries lock that device for 60 seconds. Removing the PIN opens every Manage screen again.

2Scan history & missing barcodes

Signage Scan → Scan History lists every scan with its result. Use the Missing barcodes filter to find codes that matched no product — those are items to add or barcodes to fix. You can also group by result, day or user.

ResultMeaningWhat to do
FoundThe barcode matched a product and its detail was shown.Nothing.
Not foundNo product carries that barcode.Add the product, or fix its barcode.
Off for scanA product matched, but its Show on scan is off.Turn it on if it should appear.

Clearing the history is a manager action — a multi-select delete in Odoo, or the Clear button in the app.

4Cost price & privacy

Hidden fields are filtered inside Odoo before anything is sent to a screen. Cost price is off by default. So even a curious shopper inspecting the app sees only the fields you turned on — nothing more.

Quick tips

  • The price checker needs only a name, barcode and price on a product.
  • A banner video must be a real video file (MP4, WebM, MOV…); audio files are refused.
  • Cost price is off by default and filtered on the server — hidden fields never reach the screen.
  • Show on display hides a banner; Archive removes it from the list.
  • New custom product fields appear in the scan list switched off — nothing is exposed by surprise.
  • With Same fields for all products on, per-product field changes don't show until you turn it off.
  • The Manage PIN guards the counter, not your Odoo rights.
  • Scan History → Missing barcodes is the fastest way to find items that need adding.

If something goes wrong

What you seeWhat to do
A scan shows nothingCheck the product carries that exact barcode and that Show on scan is on. Scan History will show the code as Not found or Off for scan.
A video won't play or won't saveIt must be a real video file (MP4, WebM, MOV…). An audio file such as a .ogg voice note is refused — re-upload a proper video.
A field I turned on isn't showingSame fields for all products is probably on; per-product changes don't show until it's off (Scan Fields → Same Fields for All).
A video is cut off earlyTime on Screen is Custom — switch it to Auto so the video plays its full length.
Manage won't open / asks for a PINApp Lock is on. Enter the 4-digit PIN. Five wrong tries lock the device for 60 seconds — wait, then retry.
A banner isn't on screenIt may be Off, outside its Start/End date, or Archived. Check it reads Live.
Cost price is showingTurn it off in the product's scan fields (or the global list) — it's off by default.
