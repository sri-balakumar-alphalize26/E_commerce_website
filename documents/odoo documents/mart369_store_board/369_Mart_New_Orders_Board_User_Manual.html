369 Mart New Orders Board

The Counter — New Orders Ring Until Accepted
User Manual  |  369ai

What this does

369 Mart New Orders Board puts a shop Counter in front of your orders. Once it is installed, 369 Mart → Sales → Orders opens the Counter first: a short list of the orders the shop is working on right now, with New, Quick and Express tabs. Each row says what was ordered, how it is paid and what to press next. A switch at the top takes you to All orders (the full Orders desk) and back.

A new order sets off an alarm that keeps ringing until somebody presses Accept. It rings on any backend screen, not only the Counter, and there is no off switch — the only way to quiet it is to accept the waiting orders.

Part 1 — Setting Up

Done once by an administrator. If the Orders menu opens the Counter and the ring length suits you, skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Open 369 Mart from the main menu. This module changes one entry and adds two things that show on every screen:

  • Sales → Orders — now opens the Counter. The plain Orders desk is still there: press All orders at the top of the Counter. There is only one Orders entry; the desk's own menu entry is switched off while this module is installed.
  • The “new orders” pill — a red N new orders button in the top bar, on every screen, while an order is waiting to be accepted.
  • The “New orders” notice — a pop-up that stays on screen with an Open the Counter button.

The alarm's timing is set in another app: Delivery → Configuration → Delivery Settings (step 2).

2Set how long the alarm rings

Open Delivery → Configuration → Delivery Settings and find the section The Shop Counter. The alarm rings for a while, rests, then rings again, until every new order is accepted.

  • Shop Confirms Orders — on by default. On means a paid order waits at the Counter until somebody accepts it and says it is packed; only then is a rider called.
  • Ring For (seconds) — how long one ring lasts. Default 30.
  • Alarm Repeats Every (s) — the rest between one ring and the next. Default 10.

Anything below 3 seconds is treated as 3. A change reaches every open screen at its next check, within about 10 seconds; nobody needs to reload.

Watch out: With Shop Confirms Orders off, the rider is called the moment an order is paid. Orders then never wait for Accept, so the Counter's New tab stays empty and the alarm never rings. The two ring fields are hidden while it is off.
Good to know: Changing these settings needs Delivery Manager access in the Delivery app. Counter staff do not need it.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

This is what counter staff use all day.

1The Counter

Open 369 Mart → Sales → Orders. The heading reads “New orders ring until somebody accepts them.” The list updates by itself the moment an order arrives or changes; you never need to reload.

On the screenWhat it does
Counter / All ordersThe switch between this screen and the full Orders desk (step 4).
TabsNew — orders waiting for Accept. Quick and Express — every open order of that kind, at any stage. Each tab shows its count. The Counter opens on New.
N newA red pill with the number of orders waiting for Accept. It pulses while it is not zero.
All shopsShown only when more than one shop is set up. Pick a shop to see only its orders, or All shops for everything.
Turn on the alarm / Alarm onSee step 5. Once the sound is on, the button turns into a green Alarm on label.

When a tab is empty you see “No new orders. They appear here on their own, with the alarm.” (New) or “Nothing open for Quick delivery right now.” (Quick or Express).

2Read a row

Each row is one order. From top to bottom:

  • Customer — the name, their area, and any customer tags. If your shop also takes orders on WhatsApp, a Website or WhatsApp label shows where the order came from.
  • Order line — the order number (#…), Quick or Express, the number of items and how long ago it was placed.
  • What to pack — each item with its quantity in bold.
  • Stage — a coloured tag: New order, Packing, Waiting for the rider, Rider called or Rider on the way (see the reference table).
  • waiting N min — on a New order: how long it has waited for Accept.
  • Payment — a tick with how it was paid (for example Cash collected), or Collect and the amount the rider must take at the door.
  • Rider — the rider's name, once one is set.
  • Waiting for supplier stock — the goods are being bought in (step 3).
  • Pickup code — shown once the rider is on the way.

On the right are the order total and the buttons for its stage. Click anywhere else on the row to open that order in All orders, with the order already open.

3The buttons

Which stock? Accept opens “Accept #… - from which company?”. Each company is listed with how many of each item it has and needs; only a company with every item on its shelf can be picked, and the one the order is booked to now is marked booked here now. If no company has the goods, press No stock anywhere - order from supplier. In that box you tick suppliers and press Ask these suppliers (they get a price request on WhatsApp), then type What do we pay?, optionally Which supplier?, and When will it be here?, and press Accept and order it in. The order is accepted and shows Waiting for supplier stock until you press Goods arrived.

Good to know: Buying in does not change the customer's price and sends them no new message. A promised date later than today turns the order into Express.

Cancelling. Cancel order asks “Cancel order #…?” and warns that the customer and the rider are told straight away. If the invoice is already paid, it adds that the refund is done from the Delivery form afterwards. Press Cancel the order, or Keep it to back out.

Watch out: Cancelling here does not move money. If the customer has paid, someone must still issue the refund.

4Switch to All orders

Press All orders at the top right to open the full Orders desk in place of the Counter. The desk has the same switch: press Counter to come back. Use All orders for searching, filters, delivered and cancelled orders, and everything else described in the 369 Mart Orders manual.

5The alarm, on any screen

The alarm is not tied to the Counter. Anyone with Salesperson or Website designer rights hears it on every backend screen, and it checks for waiting orders every 10 seconds (a brand-new order is usually heard at once). It rings for the Ring For time, rests for the Alarm Repeats Every time, and repeats until the last waiting order is accepted.

  • Turning on the sound — a browser will not play sound until you have clicked or pressed a key. Until then the Counter shows “No sound yet…” and a Turn on the alarm button. Any click or key press anywhere turns it on; you only do this once per visit.
  • N new orders — the red pill in the top bar. Click it to open the Counter. Before the sound is on it adds · tap to hear.
  • New orders notice — “1 order is waiting to be accepted” (or “N orders are…”) with an Open the Counter button. It stays until nothing is waiting, and is not shown while the Counter is open.
Important: There is no switch to mute it. The alarm counts every order waiting for Accept in every shop, whichever tab or shop you are looking at. Orders waiting for supplier stock do not ring.
Tip: While the Store app's own New Orders screen is open, this alarm stays silent, because that screen rings for itself. You never hear two alarms at once.

6The same Counter on the staff console

The staff console's Orders screen has the same Counter / All orders switch, the same tabs, rows and buttons, Cancel included. It reads the same orders: an order accepted on the console is accepted in the backend, and the other way round. The red N new orders pill also shows on every console page.

Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1Who can do what

RoleWhat they can do
SalespersonSees Sales → Orders, works the Counter (Accept, Packed, Put back, Goods arrived, Cancel) and hears the alarm everywhere.
Website designerHears the alarm and sees the pill on every screen, and can use the Counter on the staff console.
Delivery Manager (Delivery app)Changes the ring and pause in Delivery Settings.
Internal userNo Orders menu, no Counter and no alarm. Give Salesperson rights to work the Counter.

2What the customer and rider hear

Buttons on the Counter send messages through the Store app: the customer hears when an order is accepted and when it is packed, and the customer and rider are both told about a cancellation.

3Removing the module

Uninstalling 369 Mart: New Orders Board puts the old Sales → Orders entry back exactly as it was, opening the Orders desk directly. The pill, the notice and the app-wide alarm go away with it. No orders are changed.

Quick tips

  • Click once anywhere after signing in, so the alarm can make a sound.
  • Accept stops the alarm; packing can come later.
  • The New tab is the to-do list; Quick and Express show every open order of that kind.
  • Click a row (not a button) to open the order in All orders.
  • “Collect” on a row means the rider takes cash at the door.
  • Put back returns an order to New if it was accepted by mistake.
  • Ring length and pause live in Delivery → Configuration → Delivery Settings.
  • A cancelled paid order still needs a refund.
