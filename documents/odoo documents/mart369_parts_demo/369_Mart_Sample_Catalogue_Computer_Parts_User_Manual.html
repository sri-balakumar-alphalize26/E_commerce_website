What this does

369 Mart Sample Catalogue - Computer Parts fills the shop with a worked example of a computer parts store: 29 categories in six sections, 92 sample products with real brands and believable prices, and home page content built around them. It gives every screen something to show when you demonstrate or test the shop app and website.

It is not stock. Nothing here is ordered, counted or priced for sale: no stock quantities are loaded, and the prices are examples. It has no screens or settings of its own; you install it, look around, and uninstall it when you are done.

Watch out: The sample products are published, so they appear in the shop app and on the website like real products, with their sample prices. That is why this belongs on a demo or test database only.

What else changes while it is installed

  • Seven categories are taken over — if your database already has Storage, Peripherals, Networking, Computers, Laptops, Accessories or Chargers, the sample catalogue uses that category instead of making a second copy, so existing links keep working.
  • Grocery categories are hidden — Fresh Fruits, Daily Essentials, Home & Kitchen, Office & Stationery, Electronics and a test category are hidden from the app. They are not deleted.
  • The grocery home content is switched off — the grocery tabs, tiles, banners and rows that come with the home page are put away, so customers do not see two sets of tabs.

Where the sample home page shows

The sample tabs, banners, tiles and rows are added to the page called Example - grocery layout (not shown). As its name says, customers do not see that page unless it is switched on. A new shop opens on its own page built from its catalogue instead.

To show the sample home content, open 369 Mart → Page building → Saved Home Pages, open Example - grocery layout (not shown) and press Switch this on. To go back, open your usual page and press Switch this on there.

Good to know: Even without switching the page on, the sample categories and products are in the shop and can be browsed and searched.

When to use it

  • To demonstrate the shop app and website to a customer or a new team member.
  • To test screens, search, filters and the home page builder against a full, sensible catalogue.
  • To take screenshots or training material without using real products.

Do not use it to start a real shop: the products carry no stock and sample prices only.

How to remove it

Uninstall the module from the backend's list of apps (Administrator (Settings) rights needed). Uninstalling:

  • removes every sample product;
  • shows the hidden grocery categories again;
  • puts the grocery home content back exactly as it was.
Watch out: The categories the module created, and the seven it took over, belong to it once it is installed, so they are removed with it. If you put real products into any of them, move those products to another category first.
