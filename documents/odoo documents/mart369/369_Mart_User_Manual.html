What this does

369 Mart is the base of the whole suite. On its own it shows very little: it puts the 369 Mart menu on the main menu, keeps a few shared settings that every other 369 Mart part reads, and supplies the common pieces the other screens are built from, such as the logo picker used for categories.

Every other 369 Mart module needs this one, and each can be added or removed on its own. Because everything hangs off it, removing 369 Mart removes the whole suite.

Part 1 — Setting Up

Done once by an administrator. If the other 369 Mart parts are already set up, you can skip to Part 2.

1Where everything lives

Open 369 Mart from the main menu. This module creates the menu and six headings; the other 369 Mart modules put their screens under them:

  • Store — the shop itself, for example the store settings.
  • Sales — orders and customers.
  • Money — payments and wallets.
  • Catalogue — categories, products and searches.
  • Page building — the parts behind the home page and product page builders.
  • Advanced — the "all views" screens and extra settings each module adds.

A heading only appears when you can open at least one screen inside it, so with only the base installed there is nothing to open yet.

2The shared settings

The record called 369 Mart Settings holds what every part shares. You change these fields on screens that other 369 Mart modules add:

  • Remember for (seconds) — how long the shop app may reuse the last answer it fetched before asking again. Default 60. Set 0 while you are making changes and want to see them at once.
  • Image address — leave it empty in almost every case. Fill it in (for example with your shop's web address) only when the shop app cannot load images through the backend.
  • Alert on new orders — on by default. The staff console's bell and sidebar badge count orders waiting to be moved on.
  • Alert on low stock — on by default. The staff console's bell says how many products are at or below the level where the shop app starts saying "Only N left".

Where to find them:

  • Page building → Home Page Settings — Remember for (seconds) (under Speed) and Image address (under For whoever sets up the app). This screen comes with the 369 Mart Home Page module.
  • Store → Settings → Alerts — the New orders and Low stock switches. Press Save changes. This screen comes with the 369 Mart Payments & Wallet module.
Watch out: Remember to put Remember for (seconds) back to 60 after you finish editing. Left at 0, the shop app asks the backend again on every visit, which makes it slower.
Good to know: The two alerts are for the staff console only. Email and SMS alerts are not set up.
Part 2 — Everyday Use

The shared pieces staff meet on other 369 Mart screens.

1Choosing a category logo

When you edit a category under Catalogue → Catalog, the Logo field is this module's logo picker. It has two tabs:

  • Built-in — pick a ready-made logo. A main category offers 16 simple icons; a sub-category offers 40 drawings (keyboard, laptop, apple, soap bottle and so on). Picking one replaces any uploaded picture.
  • Upload your own — press Choose a picture (or Replace), or Remove to clear it. PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF or SVG, up to 15 MB.
Category levelHow the shop app shows the logoBest picture
Main categorySmall: 18 × 18 px beside the name on the top bar.A simple, bold mark; a PNG with a see-through background. Saved as 128 × 128.
Sub-categoryFull tile: 104 × 104 px on the home page, 88 × 88 on the category page.At least 512 × 512. Saved as 512 × 512.

Every upload opens Crop the logo. Drag the picture to move it, use the slider (or the + and − keys) to zoom, or press Show it all or Fill the square. The right side shows it As the app will show it. Press Use this logo to keep it, or Cancel.

Tip: If the cropper says the picture "will look soft", find a bigger one. A sub-category picture should be 512 × 512 or larger.

2Number boxes

Every number box on a 369 Mart screen accepts digits only. Letters such as "e" and the "+" and "−" signs are refused as you type. A box that counts things (a quantity or a number of days) also refuses the decimal point.

3The 369 Mart tab on a product

The product form gets a 369 Mart tab. This module adds it empty; the 369 Mart Home Page and Product Page modules fill it with their own fields. If you see the tab with nothing in it, neither of those modules is installed.

Part 3 — Keeping It Secure & In Order

1Which products the shop lists

Every list a shopper sees — home page rows, categories, search and offers — uses one rule: a product is listed only when it is active, published on the website, and not a service. So a wallet top-up, a gift card or a repair charge can be bought but never fills a "New this week" row.

Good to know: The rule only affects lists. A product opened directly — from the cart, a shared link or its product page — still works, so a gift card already in a basket can still be bought.

2Who can do what

RoleWhat they can do with the shared settings
Internal userRead only. Other modules read the settings for them.
Website designerRead and change the shared settings (cannot delete the record).
Administrator (Settings)Full control.

The headings themselves are not locked. Each screen under them is limited by the module that adds it, and a heading shows only when you can open something inside it, so most staff only see the headings they work in.

Quick tips

  • Install the 369 Mart Suite to get every part in one go.
  • Set Remember for (seconds) to 0 while editing, then back to 60.
  • Leave Image address empty unless images fail to load in the shop app.
  • A sub-category picture should be at least 512 × 512.
  • Picking a built-in logo replaces an uploaded one.
  • Services and unpublished products never show in shop lists.
  • An empty heading or tab means the module that fills it is not installed.
